Touch controls (phone and tablet)

Move with the joystick in the bottom-left corner. Drag anywhere else to look. FIRE shoots exactly where the dot points; headshots do double damage. To aim down the sights, double-tap the right half of the screen or tap AIM: the view zooms in, shots are tighter and you move slower. Do it again to stop aiming. RELOAD reloads before the magazine runs dry. With a melee weapon in hand, FIRE swings it at whatever is in front of you. The buttons next to FIRE switch guns and use a medkit. The top-right buttons switch the camera view and the flashlight and open SKILLS, BASE and INVENTORY. Your health bar floats above you in third person and sits under the map in first person. The city wraps around: walk or drive off one edge and you come back in on the other side. While any menu is open (inventory, trunk, trader, forge, skills, base or the game menu) a green bubble protects you: zombies already within 14 m disappear and come back 36 to 52 m away, nothing can hurt you, and any that come close are pushed out, until 1.5 seconds after you close it.

Controller

Xbox, PlayStation and most Bluetooth or USB controllers work. Left stick: move, or steer in a car. Right stick: look. Right trigger: fire, or gas in a car. Left trigger: aim down the sights (hold), or brake and reverse in a car. Right stick click: reload. A / Cross: drive, exit, repair or trade. B / Circle: medkit. X / Square: switch gun. Y / Triangle: camera view. LB: turret. RB: trap. D-pad up: flashlight. D-pad down: wall. D-pad left: base. View/Share: inventory. Start/Options: menu. D-pad right or Menu/Options: skills. In menus, the D-pad or left stick moves between buttons, A presses, B closes, and the right stick scrolls.

Keyboard and mouse (PC)

Click the game to capture the mouse (Esc lets it go). Mouse: look. Left click or Space: fire. R: reload. Right click (hold): aim down the sights, which zooms in, tightens your shots and slows you down. Z toggles aiming instead of holding. Mouse wheel or Q: switch weapon (guns and melee). WASD or the arrow keys: move; in a car W is gas and S brakes and reverses. E: drive, get out, repair, trade or forge. H: medkit. V: camera view. F: flashlight. T: turret. G: trap. X: wall. B: base. K: skills. I or Tab: inventory. M or Esc: menu.

Loot, money and the shop

Every zombie drops a few items, rarer ones less often: common scrap and ammo, uncommon (green ring) medkits, vehicle parts and fuel, rare (blue ring) gems, and epic (purple ring) teeth and special zombie parts. In multiplayer every kill drops the same loot for every player, so everyone gets their own copy, and buildings hold the same items for everyone. Anything within 5 metres of you is picked up automatically. Each backpack slot holds one stack (100 scrap, 50 gems or teeth, 25 medkits, 25 vehicle parts, 15 fuel cans, 5 of a zombie part); money and ammo take no space. Every stack over your slot limit slows you 20%, and five over pins you in place until you drop something from the INVENTORY. Upgrade the backpack at the shop for more slots. Every car also has its own trunk (sedan 125 slots, pickup 175, van 250): stand next to one or sit in it and open INVENTORY to store or take loot. Some abandoned cars still have things in the trunk. Sell it at the shop: 1000 silver = 1 gold, 1000 gold = 1 diamond. The shop is a safe zone the dead cannot enter, and you heal while you are there. It sells guns (SMG, shotgun, rifle), ammo for each, medkits, fuel, parts and upgrades.

Cars

Walk up to a car and press DRIVE. GAS and BRAKE drive it, and the joystick steers. Hold BRAKE when stopped to reverse. You can run zombies down, but it damages the car. Cars with a flat tyre or wreck damage can be repaired with three vehicle parts. Flipped cars stay wrecked.

Mods

Rebirth can be modded. Everything in the world lives in sorted content files: weapons, shop goods and loot in content/items, zombie types in content/zombies, people in content/characters, and streets, props, furniture, building types and landmark buildings in content/world. Mods go in the mods folder and can add new weapons, zombies, shop items, characters, props, furniture and buildings. Turn mods on or off under Settings. The full guide is MODDING.md in the game files.

Melee weapons and gore

The trader sells six melee weapons that never need ammo: Knife (fast), Baseball Bat (wide, knocks zombies back), Crowbar, Machete (takes arms off), Fire Axe (chops limbs and heads) and Sledgehammer (slow, bursts heads, sends zombies flying). Switch to them like any gun and swing with FIRE. Hard hits can take arms off and headshot kills can burst heads. Some zombies already carry the wounds that turned them: bites, mauled chests, old rot, missing arms. Turn gore down or off in MENU > Gore.

Survivors, death and your backpack

Walk up to a waving survivor to rescue them. With nobody rescued, dying means you are eaten and turned. Once you have rescued someone, they come and save you, but your backpack of money, loot and guns stays where you fell, marked by an orange beam, until you go back for it.

Skills and classes

Kills and rescues earn skill points. Your first point picks one of eight classes and blocks the others until you respec. Each class has 15 passives and an ultimate that unlocks after 11 points, and 46 points max out a class.

Settlements

Tap BASE to found a settlement anywhere. Rescued survivors live there and take jobs: Guard, Doctor, Runner, Farmer, Cook, Smith or Mechanic. Build houses for more room (10 to 100 survivors) and walls to slow the dead. Hordes will raid it. Engineers can lay traps and build a hidden stash.

Multiplayer

Everyone who has this game open at the same time plays in the same city. You see each other with name tags and health bars (green dots on the map), fight the same zombies and can drive past each other. Whoever lands the killing shot gets the loot. Loot, survivors, settlements and shop purchases are still your own. Only signed-in people the game is shared with can join.

Blacksmith forge

A Blacksmith carries a forge. Anyone within 25 metres of a Blacksmith (you, a survivor Smith at your settlement, or another player) gets a FORGE button and can craft rare gear from zombie parts and gems, at that Blacksmith's skill prices.

Map and GPS

The map (top left) shows cars, the shop, your base, your backpack and survivors. The GPS bar (top) shows those plus nearby zombies in the direction you are facing.
